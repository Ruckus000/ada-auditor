Coastal Depot Review

The depot lease expires within the year and the review below sets out the position ahead of the renewal decision.

Coastal has operated below its designed capacity for six consecutive quarters. The shortfall is structural rather than seasonal: the berth allocation changed when the port authority reorganised its western quay, and the depot has not recovered the volume it lost at that point.

Staffing has been held flat throughout. No redundancy programme has been proposed and none is contemplated in this review.

Two options are open. The first is renewal on the existing footprint, accepting the lower utilisation and the rent that comes with it. The second is renewal on a reduced footprint, surrendering the northern apron back to the port authority.

A third option, closure, is recorded here only to note that it has been considered and set aside for this cycle.

Note. This box straddles the gutter on purpose. It belongs to neither column, and a reading-order algorithm has to place it somewhere.
Financial position

Rent is the dominant cost and is fixed for the remainder of the term. Handling costs scale with volume and have fallen accordingly.

The reduced-footprint option saves roughly a fifth of the rent while retaining the berth access that makes the site useful at all.
