Shift Handover

Handover is spoken and then written. The written note is what the incoming lead relies on if the outgoing lead has already left.

Night shift

Night covers the yard and the indoor bays. Anything still open at dawn is named, not implied.

Outstanding jobs

Jobs that started at night and are not finished are listed with the bay, not with the person who started them.

Bay 4 conveyor

The conveyor was stopped after a belt wander. It is isolated. Restart is a day-shift decision.

Yard lighting

Two columns on the east fence are dark. Temporary lights are in place. A repair is booked, not started.

Staffing

Night ran one person short after midnight. Cover came from the standby list, not from overtime on the floor.

Cover list

The names used tonight are ticked on the office copy. Unticked names were not called.

Day shift

Day takes the outstanding jobs as written. New work is not mixed into the night list.

Opening checks

Checks are done before the first vehicle is accepted. A failed check closes the bay, not the whole yard.

Keys

Bay keys hang on the numbered board. A missing key is a failed opening check for that bay.
