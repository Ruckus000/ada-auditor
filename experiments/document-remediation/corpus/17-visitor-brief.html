Open morning · site brief

Visitor Day Brief

This brief is for staff hosting visitors, not for the visitors themselves. Times are local.

Do not enter the machine hall unescorted.

Arrival

Hosts collect their group at the gate, not at the workshop door. Late arrivals wait at reception.

Reception

Reception issues a sticker, not a pass. The sticker is dated for this morning only.

Badges

Names are written as given. No job title is added on the sticker.

Cloakroom

Coats stay in the cloakroom. Bags larger than a notebook stay there too.

Tours

Each host walks one route. Groups do not combine on the floor.

Workshop floor

The floor route stays behind the yellow line. Machines stay under power; they are not demonstrated.

Departure

Hosts return the group to reception. The gate is not used as a shortcut out.

Feedback

A comment is optional. It is written on paper, not taken as a recorded interview.

Forms

Forms are left in the tray. They are not collected from visitors at the door.

Host copy — not for visitors
