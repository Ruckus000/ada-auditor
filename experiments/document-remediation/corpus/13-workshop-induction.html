Workshop Induction Pack

This pack is given to every new starter on their first morning. It is a short outline of the day, not a substitute for the full health and safety manual held by the supervisor.

First day

The first day is spent on site. No unsupervised work is scheduled until the safety briefing has been signed.

Arrival

New starters report to the workshop office, not to the floor. A named host meets them and walks the rest of the morning with them.

Sign-in desk

The desk issues a visitor pass for the first day only. The pass is returned before leaving.

Locker keys

A locker is allocated in the changing room. Lost keys are reported to the office the same day.

Safety briefing

The briefing covers the workshop as it is today. It is repeated if the floor layout changes.

Fire exits

Two marked exits serve the main bay. The briefing walks both routes before any machine is started.

After week one

By the end of the first week the starter should have a named mentor and a short written note of what still needs practice.

Mentor pairing

Pairing is decided by the supervisor, not by the starter. The mentor is someone already signed off on the same tasks.

Review notes

The mentor keeps a one-page note of tasks watched and tasks still to watch. The note stays in the office file.

Note. PPE is issued at the locker, not at the machine.
