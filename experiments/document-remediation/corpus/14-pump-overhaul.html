Pump Overhaul Log

Entries cover one shift on the east pump. The log is written as work proceeds, not reconstructed afterwards.

Planned work

Planned items were agreed at the morning briefing. Nothing is added to this section after the briefing closes.

Seal replacement

The outboard seal is the only planned replacement this shift. The inboard seal is inspected and left in place if dry.

Tools required

Puller, torque wrench, and the seal kit staged on the bench before isolation begins.

Isolation steps

Power locked out, suction and discharge closed, casing drained. The permit is signed before the coupling is opened.

Bearing check

Both bearings are checked once the coupling is free. Play outside the maker’s limit stops the job and is written under unplanned work.

Clearance readings

Readings are entered in millimetres. A blank cell means the check was not reached, not that the reading was zero.

Unplanned work

Anything found after the briefing belongs here, even if it is finished in the same shift.

Leak reports

A leak found during isolation is reported before the seal kit is opened, so the parts list stays honest.

Immediate action

Contain first, then decide whether the overhaul continues or the pump is stood down.

Issued from stores

These lines are the consumables booked against this job, not a new section of the log.
