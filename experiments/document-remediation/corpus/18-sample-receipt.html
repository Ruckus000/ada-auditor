Sample Receipt Protocol

Samples are logged before they are stored. An unlogged sample is not placed in a fridge.

Logging

Logging is done at the bench by the receiving technician. It is not done later from memory.

Paper forms

The paper form is completed even when the digital log is available. The form stays with the sample until storage.

Batch numbers

The number on the form is copied from the sender’s label. A missing label stops receipt.

Time of receipt

Time is written as local time to the minute. Rounding to the hour is not accepted.

Digital log

The digital log is a copy of the paper form, not a second numbering scheme.

Unique ids

The system issues one id per sample. Reusing an id is treated as a failed receipt.

Storage

Storage starts only after both logs exist. Location is written on the form before the door is closed.

Fridge

The fridge holds only logged samples. Unlabelled tubes found inside are removed and held separately.

Shelf map

The map on the door shows shelf letters, not sample ids. Ids are on the form only.

Hold. Samples without a time of receipt stay on the bench.
