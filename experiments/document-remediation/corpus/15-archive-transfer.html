Draft list — boxes not yet sealed

Archive Transfer Note

This note lists what left the office and what is still to go. It is not a catalogue of the whole holding.

Incoming boxes

Boxes arriving this week are listed before they are opened. Counts are of boxes, not of files inside them.

Paper series

Paper is packed as it stood on the shelf. Folders are not reordered during packing.

Minutes

Bound minute books travel upright. Loose gatherings are wrapped before they go in a box.

Correspondence

Letter files keep their original clips. New fasteners are not added for the journey.

Photographs

Prints and negatives are packed separately even when they belong to the same job.

Prints

Each print stays in its sleeve. Sleeves are not labelled again if they already carry a number.

Outgoing boxes

Outgoing boxes are those leaving for the off-site store. A box is outgoing only after the receipt is signed.

Digital copies

A copy is made when the paper cannot travel, not as a substitute for every file.

Checksums

Each copy carries a checksum written on the transfer sheet. The checksum is the record that the copy was complete at the door.

Series. A run of records kept together because they were created by the same activity, not because they share a subject heading.
