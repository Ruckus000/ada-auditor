Summary

Coastal has operated below its designed capacity for six consecutive quarters. The shortfall is structural rather than seasonal, dating to the port authority's reorganisation of the western quay.

Two options are live: renewal on the existing footprint, or renewal on a reduced footprint surrendering the northern apron. Closure has been considered and set aside for this cycle.

The recommendation at the end of this review is the reduced footprint, on the basis of the rent saving set out under options.

Options and recommendation

  • Renew as-is. Accept lower utilisation and the full rent.
  • Reduce footprint. Surrender the northern apron; saves about a fifth of the rent.
  • Close. Considered and set aside for this cycle.

The recommendation is the reduced footprint. Supporting figures are published on the Coastal depot page, and the lease correspondence is held by the estates office.
