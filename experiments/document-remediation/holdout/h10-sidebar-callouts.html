Storage Policy

Free storage runs for five working days from discharge. Thereafter storage is charged daily at the published rate and is not pro-rated for part days.

Extensions

An extension may be granted where onward transport has failed for reasons outside the consignee's control. Requests are made to the duty officer before the free period ends.

Abandoned goods

Goods uncollected after ninety days are treated as abandoned and are disposed of under the authority's standing procedure. Proceeds are set against outstanding charges.

Hazardous consignments

Hazardous consignments are not eligible for free storage and are charged from the hour of discharge.

At a glanceFive working days free, then charged daily. No part-day pro-rating.
Worked exampleA container discharged on Monday and collected the following Wednesday attracts three chargeable days.
