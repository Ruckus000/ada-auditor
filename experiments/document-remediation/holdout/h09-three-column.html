Port Bulletin

Issue 214 · circulated to berth holders

Dredging

The western approach will be dredged over six nights beginning on the fourteenth. Vessels drawing more than eight metres should expect a revised allocation for that period.

Gate hours

Gate hours revert to the winter schedule at the end of the month. The night gate closes an hour earlier from that date and reopens at the usual time.

Notice to berth holders. This banner spans all three columns and interrupts the flow. Text continues in the left column beneath it.

Charges

Demurrage is unchanged. Storage charges are subject to the quarterly review and berth holders will be notified separately.

Contact

The duty officer is reachable through the usual channel at all hours during the dredging programme.
