Berth Allocation Procedure

This procedure governs how berths are allocated when demand exceeds availability. It is written in a house style that gives headings no visual weight beyond capitalisation.

Requesting a berth

Requests are made through the authority's booking desk not less than seventy-two hours before the intended arrival. Late requests are considered only where a berth is unused.

Late requests

A late request does not create a priority claim and is not recorded against the allocation history used to settle disputes.

Priority

Where two requests compete, the earlier booking prevails. Where bookings were made in the same hour, the vessel with the shorter expected turnaround is allocated first.

Disputes

Disputes are settled by the estates officer on the allocation history alone. No appeal sits above that decision within this procedure.
