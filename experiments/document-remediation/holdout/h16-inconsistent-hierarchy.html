Operations Manual Extract

The four section headings below are all at the same logical level despite being styled four different ways, and the two subsection headings are styled differently from each other.

Receiving

Consignments are checked against the manifest at the gate before entering the yard.

Discrepancies

A discrepancy is logged at the gate and does not stop the consignment entering.

Storage

Consignments are placed by class. Hazardous goods go to the segregated area.

Segregation

The segregated area is inspected daily and its register is held by the duty officer.

Dispatch

Dispatch is against a released order only. Verbal releases are not accepted.

Records

Gate logs are retained for six years and are the record of first resort in a dispute.
