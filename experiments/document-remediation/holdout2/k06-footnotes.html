Screening Coverage Note

Coverage across the three programmes reached 74.2%1 in the year to March, against a target of 80%. The shortfall is concentrated in the bowel programme2, where the invitation cycle was extended.

Cervical coverage held at 71.8%, unchanged within rounding3.

1 Weighted by eligible population at the mid-year estimate.

2 Bowel screening moved from a two-year to a 30-month cycle in the prior year; the two figures are not directly comparable.

3 Rounding applied at one decimal place throughout.
