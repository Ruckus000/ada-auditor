Escalation Protocol

Three levels of nesting, with the innermost level carrying no list marker.

  1. Assess the patient and record observations.
    • If NEWS2 is 5 or above, escalate immediately.
    • If NEWS2 is 3 or 4, repeat within the hour.
      • Record the repeat even when unchanged.
      • Note who was informed and when.
  2. Contact the responsible clinician.
    • Out of hours, contact the on-call registrar instead.
  3. Document the outcome before the end of the shift.

The protocol is reviewed annually.
