Glossary of Reporting Terms

Terms are set bold at the left margin with their definitions indented beneath. Two terms carry more than one definition.

Episode
A continuous period under the care of one consultant.
Spell
A continuous period as an admitted patient, from admission to discharge.
In the community dataset, a spell instead means a referral-to-discharge period, which is not the same thing and is not interchangeable.
Coverage
The proportion of an eligible population screened within the programme interval.
Reported as a percentage weighted by the mid-year population estimate.
Breach
A wait exceeding the applicable standard.
