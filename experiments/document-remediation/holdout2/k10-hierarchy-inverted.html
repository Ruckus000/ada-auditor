Infection Control Manual

The heading sizes in this document run in the wrong direction throughout. The title is the smallest text on the page and the third-level headings are the largest.

Hand hygiene

Decontamination is required before and after every patient contact.

Alcohol gel

Gel is not effective against spore-forming organisms.

Exceptions

Soap and water is required after contact with a patient with diarrhoea.

Isolation

Side rooms are allocated by the infection prevention team.

Cohorting

Cohorting is permitted where side rooms are exhausted.
