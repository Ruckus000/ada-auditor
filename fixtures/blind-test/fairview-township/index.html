Office hours

The clerk's office is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm, and closed on statutory holidays. Building inspections are booked through the permits desk.

Council

Five councillors and a mayor, elected for four-year terms. Minutes of every regular meeting are posted within ten business days.
