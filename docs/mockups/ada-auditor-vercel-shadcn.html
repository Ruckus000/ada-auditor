What happens during a run?
  1. 1Open the selected environment.
  2. 2Walk through the selected journey.
  3. 3Capture browser and accessibility evidence.
  4. 4Apply deterministic rules and explain the result.

Possible results

  • Pass — complete evidence, no critical rule-based issue.
  • Fail — complete evidence, at least one critical issue.
  • Inconclusive — not enough evidence to judge.

ADA Auditor reports accessibility risk. It is not legal certification.
