|---|---|:--:|

**Five are blocked by alt text**, and [Brief B](vlm-scale-results.md) closed that
under zero human input: a 7B model gets the facts right and fabricates a
regulatory requirement under a real department's name, undetectably. Scale makes
it worse, not better.

**Four are blocked by nothing but a document title** — one field, and the closest
thing to a free win this project has ever had. So I checked whether the title
could be *copied* from the document's own first heading, which would be
transcription rather than invention and would clear all four.

`[V]` **It cannot. All four produce zero headings.**

```
fordcity-fee-schedule   headings=0     newcastle-pc-hearing  headings=0
nola-cpc-notice         headings=0     tml-statutes-table    headings=0
(ct-legal-notice, for contrast: 8 headings, H1 first)
```

This is open question 2 — *demotion-only caps what we can deliver* — landing
exactly where it costs the most. `Headings.java` can demote and never promote, so
a document whose headings were never tagged gets zero headings from us, and a
document with zero headings has no title to copy. **The two cheapest-looking wins
in the project are blocked by the same missing capability.**

## 2. Where the goal and the constraint have come apart

This is the thing I most need to put in front of you, and it is arithmetic rather
than opinion.

[legal-standard.md](legal-standard.md) sets the north-star metric from market
economics:

> **North-star metric: human minutes per page. Target under 5.**
> At **$8/page** against a **$50/hr** loaded remediator: break-even is ~9.6
> minutes per page, and a 50% gross margin needs **under ~4.8 minutes per page**.

**The business case needs under ~4.8 minutes per page. Zero human input needs
zero.** Those are different targets, and every wall we have hit this week sits in
the gap between them:

| wall | minutes of human work it actually represents |
|---|---|
| document title, 4 docs | **~15 seconds each.** Read the top of page 1, type it. |
| alt text, 5 docs | ~1–2 min per meaningful image, and it is the skilled part |
| heading promotion | minutes, and it is the expensive part |

`legal-standard.md` already said this in plain terms and we did not act on it:
*"A title is one field. Alt text is a person writing a sentence per meaningful
image — skilled work, but human work, not a technical barrier."*

**Zero-human is a strictly harder target than the business requires.** That may
still be the right target — there are good reasons to want it, and it is your
call, not mine. But it should be chosen knowingly, and right now it is costing us
four documents that a person could clear in a minute between them.

## 3. The options, honestly

### A — Source-first remediation
Client sends the `.docx`; we remediate the **source** and export.

- **`[V]` The mechanism works.** A native ODF source exports `/H1 /H2 /H3`
  intact, and header cells get correct scope. [Brief A's](source-export-results.md)
  kill fired on an HTML-import quirk in our own fixtures, not on the exporter.
- **`[V]` Failures are omissions, not assertions** — the exporter copies what is
  there and stays silent about what is not. That clears gate 1 by construction,
  which no inference path can do at any accuracy.
- **`[V]` All four title-blocked documents name Word upstream.** Their titles and
  headings exist in a source file.
- **Unknown, and it is the whole risk:** do clients still *have* the sources? A
  producer string proves a source existed at export time, not that anyone can
  find it now. And if the source has no alt text, the export omits it and the
  document still fails 1.1.1 — better-failing, not passing.
- **Cost:** author `.docx`/`.fodt` corpus equivalents, re-run Arm L. One session.
- **Kills it:** structure does not survive from a native source at corpus scale,
  or a client discovery conversation says the sources are gone.

### B — Human-in-the-loop at the written north star
Automate everything safe, abstain everywhere else, and hand back a question list.

- **`[V]` It already exists.** `manifest.mjs` maps omissions to questions: **22
  answers close 11 of 28** development documents.
- **`[V]` The economics support it** and it is what the market pays for.
- **`[V]` Alt text becomes tractable** — the human supplies the purpose, which is
  the one thing no model can be trusted to assert.
- **Costs:** it is a different product. Needs a review UI, a workflow, and a
  person. It abandons zero-human.
- **Kills it:** measured minutes per page come in above ~4.8.

### C — Narrow zero-human product to a document class
Ship for the classes that need nothing we cannot do.

- **`[V]` Measured today: that class is empty.** 0 of 9. **This option is dead**,
  and it is dead on evidence rather than on judgement. Recording it so it is not
  proposed again.

### D — Stop
Always on the table. The case for it is weaker than a week ago, not stronger: we
now know the exporter path works mechanically and we know precisely which four
documents are one field away.

## 4. Recommendation

**Run A next, and run it as one timeboxed brief.** It is the only live path to
zero human input, the mechanism is already verified, and it costs one session to
find out. It also attacks the title blocker and the heading blocker with the same
move, which nothing else does.

**Then decide A or B on evidence**, not before. If sources survive export at
corpus scale, A is the product and it is genuinely zero-human on our side. If
they do not, B is the product and the north star was always <5 minutes.

**Do not build anything from B yet.** The manifest exists; that is enough until A
returns.

## 5. Not doing, and why — so this is not rediscovered

| | |
|---|---|
| **A bigger or newer VLM** | `[V]` Closed by Brief B. Scale moved the failure from visible to undetectable. More scale is worse, not better. |
| **Alt text by any model, under zero-human** | `[V]` Closed. Not capability — verifiability. |
| **Typographic or layout-model heading promotion** | `[V]` Both killed and recorded. |
| **A narrow zero-human document class** | `[V]` Measured empty today. |
| **Fixing contrast** | Changing a client's colours is changing their design. Detect and report only. |
| **Font-level failures** (`7.21.*`) | `[V]` No WCAG counterpart. Commercial risk, not legal. Price it, do not build it. |
| **Holdout 2** | Sealed. Both checkpoints unspent. |

## 6. The stopping rule

**One brief, one session, then a decision: A, B, or stop.** No third exploratory
round without one of those three being chosen first.

The failure mode this project has repeated is that work continues because there
are interesting questions, and there are always interesting questions. There are
several on the FINDINGS lists right now — `PDFUACompliance`, the 7 figure
assertions, 4-bit VLM inference, per-photograph scoring. **None of them changes
the choice between A and B**, which is why none of them is scheduled.
