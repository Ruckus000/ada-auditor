# Stage 1 Round 11 Implementation Plan — the under-title line, and the operating threshold

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Remove adapter-r10's one systematic genuine false positive (the line directly under a title: cover date, subtitle, "submit to", effective date), and turn the coverage curve into a registered operating threshold with a per-document guarantee, so Stage 1 can close on a candidate the product can run.

**Architecture:** Task 1 is a prompt-fact change, not a retrain: the card prompt gains one fact, `after_h1: yes|no` (the card sits directly below a level-1 heading on the same page), computed from the same key ladder the prompt already carries, so the model can learn that this position is usually a subtitle. It is retrained once (r11) on `sft-r10` with the new fact. Task 2 is measurement only: on the folded labels, pick the score threshold by the registered rule and report the per-document clean rate, so the product's abstention setting is a recorded number, not a judgement.

**Tech Stack:** as round 10.

**Spec:** `2026-09-17-stage1-round10.md` (Global Constraints carry over), `heading-stage1-r10-2026-09-17-results.md` (§5 finding: 6 of 7 genuine FP are the under-title line; coverage table), roadmap Stage 1 bar and abstention channel.

## Global Constraints

- Round 10's Global Constraints apply verbatim (split, no test, label sources, level policy, c5 excluded, 15 h gate, commit discipline).
- **Registered before Task 1 runs:**
  - Fact: `after_h1` is true when the card's page equals the previous card's page (reading order by page, then y0) and the previous card is a key heading of level 1 in the training labels; at inference on validation, "previous card is H1" comes from the model's own prior decision on that page (the approved-stack mechanism already in the prompt), never from the label. In training, the measured population is 528 train cards directly under an H1 (H 275, P 181, Other 50, other types 22), so the fact is informative but not decisive.
  - Data: `sft-r11` = `sft-r10` rows re-emitted with the fact; row count unchanged (4,942). Leak check as before.
  - Config: identical to r10, `--iters` = 2 × rows, 15 h gate at step 100.
  - Prediction (r10 → r11, labels-audited-r10, r10v rates): genuine FP of the under-title shape fall from 6 to ≤ 2 on validation; direct FP ≤ 0.035; estimated FN does not rise above 0.125; regular-weight recall stays ≥ 0.90; coverage at score ≥ 0.99 stays ≥ 0.75.
  - Kill: if the fact fires on fewer than 40 validation cards, it cannot be measured and Task 1 is recorded as inert without training.
- **Registered before Task 2 runs:**
  - Threshold rule: the lowest score threshold at which the covered subset's direct accuracy lower bound (exact 95 %) is ≥ 0.98 and the FP upper bound is ≤ 0.02 on labels-audited-r10; report coverage and the per-document clean rate (share of validation documents with zero covered errors) at that threshold, for r10 and r11.
  - The Stage 1 bar (0.99 / 0.01 with bounds) is reported at every threshold; if no threshold reaches it, the record says so and the roadmap's Stage 1 status line records the best covered point instead.
